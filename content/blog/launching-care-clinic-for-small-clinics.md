On 4 October 2026, CARE Clinic was launched at IMA House in Ernakulam, during the installation of IMA Kochi's new office bearers. Kerala's Health Minister, K Muraleedharan, attended the event. CARE Clinic packages CARE for a single clinic, so a doctor or clinic owner can download it, install it and run the whole clinic from one computer.

## The whole clinic on one computer

CARE Clinic is free, open source clinic management software. It covers the work a small clinic does every day: appointments and a visit queue, patient records, prescriptions, lab orders and results, pharmacy and stock, and billing. Forms can be configured for the clinic and its specialty without changing CARE's core.

There are no licence fees and no subscription. The clinic holds the database and the backups on a computer it controls, and other staff computers join over the clinic's own network. After an online setup, the core workflows keep running without an internet connection.

## Built for clinics that hospital systems leave out

Most HMIS products are built for hospitals, with the servers, IT staff and budgets that come with them. Small clinics are often left with paper or with tools that lock their data away. CARE Clinic gives them the same standards based platform that runs in hospitals and public health programmes, sized for a single clinic.

## Launching with the medical community

Launching at IMA Kochi's own event reflects OHC's close work with IMA Kochi and its network of doctors and hospitals. Those doctors are the people CARE Clinic is built for, and their feedback will shape how it grows.
