The Centre for AI in Keralam (CAIK) is an AI Centre of Excellence in Thiruvananthapuram, set up through a partnership between the IndiaAI Mission under MeitY and the Government of Kerala. In September, OHC was selected for CAIK's First Cohort 2026, as part of its Acceleration programme. The OHC team received the selection letter from P K Kunhalikutty, Kerala's Minister for Industries, Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence.

## Filly and assistive AI

OHC's place in the cohort centres on Filly, CARE's ambient clinical documentation tool. Filly listens to the consultation between doctor and patient and drafts structured CARE fields for the clinician to review and submit. Filly Vision extends the same idea to other data entry work, such as reading photographed lab results and helping with registration.

The approach is assistive by design. Filly works inside clinical workflows, writes into structured and coded records, and leaves the final decision with the clinician.

## A not for profit in an AI cohort

OHC joins the cohort as a not for profit foundation rather than a startup. The cohort is a chance to take CARE's AI work further with the support of a state backed centre of excellence, while keeping it open and built for public health.
