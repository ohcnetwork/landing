The OHC team worked with the National Health Authority (NHA) to revamp the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) sandbox portal. The revamped, AI powered sandbox lets digital health integrators use agentic AI and coding agents to build, test and complete their M1 to M4 integrations in hours instead of weeks. From 5 to 8 October 2026, the team demonstrated the portal to multiple integrators at meetings in Delhi and Hyderabad, gathered their feedback, and connected with organisations exploring integration with ABDM.
