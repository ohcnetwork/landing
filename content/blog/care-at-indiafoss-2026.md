IndiaFOSS 2026, the annual free and open source software festival run by the FOSS United community, took place on 26 and 27 September at the NIMHANS Convention Centre in Bengaluru. The OHC team set up a stall to exhibit CARE and connect with the wider open source community.

## CARE on the exhibition floor

At the stall, the team walked visitors through CARE and how it is used in hospitals and public health programmes. For a platform built in the open, IndiaFOSS is where new contributors, partners and users often meet the project for the first time, and the two days helped OHC expand its connections across the open source community.

## Maintainer of the year

At the FOSS Awards, presented by the FOSS United Foundation during the festival, OHC's CTO, Vignesh Hari, was recognised as maintainer of the year for his contributions. Maintainership is the steady work that keeps an open source project dependable: reviewing contributions, shipping releases and holding a growing codebase together. The award recognises that work on CARE.
