In September, OHC and GitHub hosted two Dev Days, in Ernakulam on 12 September and in Calicut on 19 September. Both events focused on a practical question for developers: how AI agents can make building software easier and more efficient.

## AI agents in everyday development

The sessions showed how AI agents fit into the day to day work of building software, from writing and reviewing code to working through problems faster. The focus stayed on real use rather than theory, so developers could see where agents genuinely save time.

## Growing the contributor community

CARE is open source, and its progress depends on the people who contribute to it. Hosting the Dev Days with GitHub helped OHC meet new developers, build connections across the local tech community and invite more contributors into CARE.
