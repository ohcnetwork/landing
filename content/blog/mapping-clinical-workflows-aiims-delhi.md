On 7 October 2026, the OHC team visited the All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), Delhi, following a request from the institute to understand how CARE could support hospital operations and workflows in its Paediatrics and Gynaecology departments. The team spent time with each department to identify how patients move through it and what information is recorded at each step, and then looked at where CARE could make that flow easier. Since the visit, the AIIMS team has been onboarded to CARE sandbox instances, and work has started on testing CARE live to see how well it fits the departments' needs.
