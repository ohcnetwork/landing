In July, the OHC team visited Tamil Nadu to discuss how CARE could support the state's public health system. The team met Dr. K G Arunraj, Minister for Health, Medical Education and Family Welfare, along with the National Health Mission (NHM) Tamil Nadu team. The conversations covered two areas: CARE as the hospital management information system (HMIS) for government health facilities across the state, and CARE as the platform for Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam.

## One HMIS across government facilities

The first part of the discussion looked at CARE as a common HMIS for government health facilities across Tamil Nadu. A shared system means patient records, registrations, pharmacy, lab and billing follow the same structure from one facility to the next, which gives the state a consistent view of how care is being delivered.

## Supporting Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam

Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam (MTM) is Tamil Nadu's doorstep healthcare initiative, delivering screening and care to people in their homes. The second part of the discussion focused on how CARE could support MTM's field work, from home based screening to following up the people who need continued care.

CARE's work with palliative care in Kerala is relevant here. The Kerala Palliative Care Grid runs on CARE and supports home visits by field teams, which is close to how MTM reaches people.
